01 / About

A little about me.

I am interested in the intersections between technology, politics and culture, and how the forces shaping our world affect the people living in it. Through journalism and research, I enjoy exploring unfamiliar subjects, speaking to people with different perspectives and making complex developments accessible to a wider audience. I have written for The Boar, the University of Warwick's student newspaper, and have experience in content, communications and research through my work with TEDxWarwick and other projects.
